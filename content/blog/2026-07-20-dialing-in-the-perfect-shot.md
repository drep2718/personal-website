I've been chasing the same thing every morning for months: a clean, sweet
espresso shot that pulls in about **25 seconds**. It sounds simple. It is not.

## The variables never stop

Espresso is a system with far too many knobs:

- Grind size — the big one, and the one that drifts daily
- Dose — a tenth of a gram actually matters
- Tamp — consistency beats force
- Temperature and pressure — where the machine earns its keep

Change one and the others shift under you. Dial the grind finer and suddenly
the shot chokes; back off and it gushes. It's a tiny feedback loop you run
with your hands before you've had any caffeine.

## What it's really teaching me

The thing I keep coming back to is *patience*. You can't rush the loop — you
change one variable, taste, and wait for tomorrow. It's the same discipline
that makes debugging or a hard climb work: isolate one thing, observe, adjust.

The latte art is still a disaster. But the shots are getting good.
